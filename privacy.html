Privacy

How we handle your information.

Last updated: 6 August 2026

This is written to be read, not to cover anyone's back. It says what we collect, why, and what we never do with it. If anything here is unclear, ask.

The short version

  • We only ever have your email address, and your name if Stripe passes it along with a purchase.
  • We never sell it, rent it, or hand it to anyone to advertise at you.
  • There are no analytics scripts, no ad pixels, and we set no cookies.
  • Unsubscribe works, and if you ask us to delete you, we delete you.

What we collect, and why

If you request the free page: your email address, which you type into the form. The form is processed by Formspree, who deliver it to our inbox. We use it to send you the file and, occasionally, short emails about getting real work out of the AI you already pay for. Every one has an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing actually works.

If you buy something: payment is handled entirely by Stripe. Your card details go to Stripe, not to us; we never see or store them. Stripe tells us your name, email and what you bought, which we need to deliver the files and honour the refund promise.

Just visiting: this site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, keeps standard server logs (including IP addresses) that we do not control or see. The page fonts load from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google's servers when the page loads. That is the whole list.

What we never do

  • Sell or share your details with anyone for marketing
  • Run analytics, tracking pixels or advertising scripts
  • Set cookies
  • Email you more than is useful, or make leaving hard

How long we keep it

Your email stays on the list until you unsubscribe or ask to be removed. Purchase records stay as long as UK tax rules make us keep them (six years), because that part is not optional.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. For us that is a small job, so just ask and it will be done quickly. If you think we have handled your information badly you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk, though we would rather you told us first so we can fix it.

How to reach us

Reply to any email we have sent you, or write to connor@heoka.co.uk. A person reads it, and that person is the one who runs the place.
