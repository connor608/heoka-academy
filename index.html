Questions

The ones we get asked.

Do I need to pay for anything else?

No. You need the ChatGPT or Claude subscription you already have, and Obsidian, which is free. There is no Heoka subscription and nothing to renew. One payment and it is yours.

I'm not technical. Can I actually do this?

Yes, and there is nothing in here that looks like code. The hardest thing you will do is copy a folder and install a free app. If you get stuck, reply to the delivery email and I will get you going.

Does it work on Windows? On my phone?

Windows, Mac, both fine. Your notes sync to your phone through Obsidian if you want them there, though you will do the setup on a computer.

Isn't this just prompts? I can find prompts for free.

You can, and most of them are rubbish because they know nothing about you. The prompts are the small half of this. The valuable half is the business file and the folder underneath, which is what makes the same prompt give you an answer about your business instead of a business.

What's the difference between the kit and the playbook?

The kit is hands-on: you set it up on a Sunday and your AI starts answering as though it knows your business. The playbook is the thinking layer for an owner with staff: where your hours go, what to hand over first, what it is worth, and how to make sure client information is not quietly ending up somewhere it should not. Most people start with the kit. The bundle is there because they are better together and it is only twenty pounds more.

Why is the kit only £29?

Because this is the start of the road, not the end of it. Some people will want the course later, and some will eventually want us to build the private version that lives in their office. £29 makes trying it an easy decision, and I would rather have ten thousand people using this than three hundred.

Is my business information safe in this?

The notes sit on your own computer as ordinary files. What you paste into ChatGPT goes to ChatGPT, exactly as it does today, so nothing gets worse. If you reach a point where that matters, that is a real conversation and it is at the bottom of this page.
