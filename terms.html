These are the terms for buying from Heoka Academy. They are short because the deal is simple: you pay once, you get the files, and if it does not help you, you get your money back.

What you are buying

Digital files: templates, guides and a ready-made folder structure, delivered as downloads. One payment, no subscription, nothing renews. Once you have bought something, updates we make to it are free; the download page and your delivery email always have the latest version.

Delivery

Payment is handled by Stripe. As soon as it goes through you are sent to the download page, and the same links arrive by email. If anything fails to arrive, reply to the receipt and a person will send the files over directly.

The refund promise

Run one job from the kit. If your AI does not answer better than it did before, reply to the delivery email and you will be refunded in full. No form, no questions, and you keep the files. That is deliberately better than the legal minimum, so nothing in these terms takes away from it. Your statutory rights as a UK consumer are unaffected by anything on this page.

What you can do with the files

  • Use them in your own business, and with your own team, as much as you like
  • Adapt them, rewrite them, and build on them for your own work

What we ask you not to do: resell the files, republish them, or pass copies around outside your business. The kit is priced so that buying it is an easy decision; please let other people make that decision themselves.

Sensible expectations

The kit gives your AI a memory and a set of working methods. It does not run your business, and nothing in it is legal, financial or professional advice for your specific situation. What you get out of it depends on what you put in, which the sales page says in plain sight.

Other people's tools

ChatGPT, Claude and Obsidian are other companies' products with their own terms and prices. We are not affiliated with any of them, and if one of them changes what their product does, that is outside our control, though we do keep the kit updated as the tools move.

The legal frame

These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales. If a dispute ever gets that far, something has gone badly wrong; email first and it will almost certainly be sorted the same day.
